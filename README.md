# Paykit Website

### Fintech Website Design & Front-End Development Case Study

A responsive corporate and product website developed for **Paykit Kenya**, a digital payments platform serving individuals, merchants and businesses.

## Overview

The Paykit website project focused on creating a modern, accessible and commercially focused digital presence for a growing Kenyan fintech brand.

The website was designed to communicate Paykit's payment products clearly while providing different customer segments with an easy way to understand the platform, explore available services and take action.

The project combined:

- Front-end web development
- UI/UX design
- Responsive design
- Brand implementation
- Product communication
- Search engine optimization
- Website performance optimization

## The Challenge

Financial products can easily become difficult to understand when users are presented with too much technical or transactional information.

The website therefore needed to communicate multiple Paykit products while remaining:

- Simple
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Responsive
- Easy to navigate
- Consistent with the Paykit brand

The design approach focused on presenting each product around a clear customer problem and benefit rather than overwhelming visitors with technical details.
